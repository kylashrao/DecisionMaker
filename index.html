Frequently Asked Questions

Is the wheel completely random?

Yes, the wheel uses pseudo-random algorithms combined with random starting torque to guarantee unbiased outcomes.

Can I use custom text items?

Absolutely! Simply modify the text box on the right panel with comma-separated values to create personalized wheels.

Is this tool free?

Yes, DecisionMaker.io is 100% free to use for personal, educational, and professional decisions.

About DecisionMaker.io

DecisionMaker.io is a lightweight, ultra-fast online utility platform designed to solve daily micro-dilemmas instantly. Whether you are settling yes/no questions, picking classroom student names, or choosing what to eat for dinner, our interactive spinners make decision-making fast, fun, and fair.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. DecisionMaker.io does not store custom choices or user data on external servers; all spin calculations occur directly within your web browser.

We use third-party advertising companies (such as Google AdSense) to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use information about your visits to provide relevant advertisements.
